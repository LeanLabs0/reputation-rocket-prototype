Reputation Rocket V2: reviews that name the category, the buyer and the problem

V1 drafts can only use what the customer happens to say, and most customers never mention the category, who they are or what they used before. V2 adds a few taps to the same chat that ask for exactly that, in the customer's own picks or words. Those are the facts behind a "Best X for Y with Z" answer from an AI.

27 real V1 sessions (Aug to Oct 2026), scored with a script. 11 September Trustpilot reviews, read 10/16. 18 of 27 did name a feature, mostly forms, so V1 is good at features and weak at category, buyer and alternatives.

What V2 changes

  • Asks "What do you use it for?" as a multi-select of the services the company actually sells, so the customer can say what they bought in one tap.
  • Quick taps on how it went, who the customer is and what they used before.
  • eimmigration, preview 2: starts with who the customer is, what they used before and the problem. Then the services, where they see results (only results tied to those services) and a 1 to 5 rating for each result. Choices sit inside the chat, under the question.
  • The draft uses a phrase only when the customer picked it. Choice questions can be skipped or answered in the customer's own words, and nothing starts pre-selected.
  • The draft only recommends, praises or says "nothing to add" when the customer's own answers say so, and low ratings get the same questions as high ones.
  • eimmigration, under 5 stars: the customer finishes the chat and is offered a follow-up, but gets no drafts or review links. The review is saved and the team reaches out first.
  • A 5-star chat ends on V1 next to V2, scored on whether the review names the category, the buyer and the problem. Under 5 stars, the eimmigration preview ends on the thank-you screen. Reviewers can still open the saved drafts and that comparison.
